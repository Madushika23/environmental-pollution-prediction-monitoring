Air Quality

Predicts PM2.5 and pollution category from weather and pollutant readings.

PM2.5 refers to fine particulate matter 2.5 micrometers or smaller — small enough to enter the lungs and bloodstream, which is why it's the primary indicator used worldwide for air quality health risk. This model takes ten weather and pollutant readings and predicts a PM2.5 value (in µg/m³, micrograms per cubic meter), then classifies it into one of six standard air quality categories (Good through Hazardous) using international WHO/EPA breakpoints. Live mode fetches current conditions for either of Sri Lanka's two official monitoring locations; Demo mode lets you type in any values to test the model. The system also auto-collects a fresh Live reading for both stations every 15 minutes in the background, independent of anyone using the site.
