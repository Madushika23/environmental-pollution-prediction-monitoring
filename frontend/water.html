Water Quality

Predicts drinking-water safety from temperature, conductivity, and pH.

This model takes temperature, conductivity, and pH and classifies the water as Safe or Unsafe to drink. It was trained to approximate a rule that also considers dissolved oxygen and fecal coliform — those need lab testing, so the live model works from the three values a low-cost sensor could realistically provide.

  1. 1 Check the IoT Live tab for real sensor data, or use Demo to type in your own values.
  2. 2 Get a Safe/Unsafe prediction.
  3. 3 IoT readings come from the ESP32 sensor board automatically; a snapshot is saved every 15 minutes. Demo readings are not saved.
